CYCLINGOS ZWIFT LINK: SETUP (about 5 minutes, once)

You need: your Mac, Zwift on the same Mac, your phone with the CyclingOS app, and both on the same Wi-Fi.

1. UNZIP
   Double-click the zip you downloaded. Move the new folder "CyclingOS Zwift Link" to your Desktop.

2. FIRST OPEN (this is the only awkward part)
   Open the folder. RIGHT-CLICK (or hold Control and click) "Start Zwift Link", choose Open,
   then click Open again in the box that appears.
   macOS asks this once because the program was not downloaded from the App Store. It is safe.
   If macOS asks to let Terminal access your Desktop or Downloads, click OK.

3. A Terminal window opens, then a web page (the "dashboard"). Keep the Terminal window open.
   If macOS asks whether "node" may accept incoming connections, click ALLOW.
   (If you clicked Deny by mistake: System Settings > Network > Firewall > Options, set node to Allow.)

4. SIGN IN
   In the dashboard, type your Zwift email and password under "Zwift account" and click Save & connect.
   They are stored only on this Mac.

5. START ZWIFT
   Open Zwift and start a ride. The dashboard checklist turns green as things connect.
   If it still says "waiting" after a minute, quit Zwift completely and open it again.

6. CONNECT YOUR PHONE
   Phone on the same Wi-Fi. Open the CyclingOS app > Zwift live > choose your Mac.
   Type the pairing code shown on the dashboard (under "Phone pairing"), then press Start ride.

NEXT TIME: just double-click "Start Zwift Link" (no right-click needed), open Zwift, ride.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
   On the dashboard, click "Show log file" at the bottom and send that file to the person who gave you this.
   To stop everything at any time, click the red "Stop everything" button.
