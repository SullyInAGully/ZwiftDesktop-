Getting started

  1. Sign in to Zwift in the box below. Your password stays on this computer.
  2. Open Zwift and start a ride (any route). If your computer asks whether “node” may accept incoming connections, choose Allow.
  3. On your phone (same Wi-Fi as this computer): open the CyclingOS app, go to Zwift live, pick this computer and type the pairing code shown below.
  4. When the checklist below is green, press Start ride in the phone app.
Keep the Terminal window open while you ride. Closing it stops the connection.

Phone app steps

1. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi network as this computer.
2. In the app, open Zwift live and pick “CyclingOS Zwift” (allow local network access when asked), or enter the address shown above.
